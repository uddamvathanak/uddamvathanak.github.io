01 / Introduction

A little about me.

Connecting machine learning with biological research.

My current research focus is real-world models. My broader interests span foundation models and single-cell omics, with research contributions to spatial transcriptomics and the DISCO platform. I maintain the DISCO website, helping researchers find its atlases, analysis tools, and toolkit resources. I also share projects in language models, research assistants, and Python education.

I studied at Monash University from 2019 to 2021, receiving a Summer Research Scholarship. I enjoy sharing practical resources and learning through hands-on experiments.

For research collaborations, project discussions, and professional introductions, connect with me on LinkedIn.

DISCO.

Deeply Integrated human Single-Cell Omics

I maintain the DISCO website, an entry point to human single-cell atlases and tools for exploring and interpreting cellular data.

The portal brings together DISCO V1 and V2 resources, including cell type annotation, mapping, enrichment, integration, and toolkits.
