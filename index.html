01 / Introduction

A little about me.

Connecting machine learning with biological research.

My current research focus is world models and simulation. My broader interests span foundation models and single-cell omics, with research contributions to spatial transcriptomics and the DISCO platform. I maintain the DISCO website, helping researchers find its atlases, analysis tools, and toolkit resources. My CellGen research explores computational models of cellular responses to perturbations within the AI in Drug Discovery (AIDD) programme.

I studied at Monash University from 2019 to 2021, receiving a Summer Research Scholarship. I enjoy sharing practical resources and learning through hands-on experiments.

For research collaborations, project discussions, and professional introductions, connect with me on LinkedIn.

03 / Current research

CellGen &
world models.

Research in progress

Modelling how cells respond to change.

I’m developing CellGen to model cellular responses to perturbations using single-cell RNA sequencing data. My CellGen work sits within the AI in Drug Discovery (AIDD) programme.

This work connects with my current focus on world models and simulation: learning representations of cellular states and studying how those states may change under different perturbations. The longer-term aim is to support predictive modelling for biological research and drug discovery.

Cellular state+Perturbation→Predicted response

An ongoing research direction, with models and evaluation under development.
